Rules:
- Use null for anything the goal and plan summary do not support. Do not invent response times; use null unless the engineer states one.
- Convert business volumes to rates using only numbers the engineer gave. Show each step of the arithmetic in "reasoning".
- If the engineer gives a peak multiplier (for example "2x last year"), apply it and say so in "reasoning".
- Prefer "iterations_per_second" when the goal is stated in business transactions or journeys, and "samples_per_second" when it is stated in requests, queries or calls.
- "samples_per_vcpu" is the expected sustainable samples per second per load generator vCPU at about 75% CPU. - "risks" lists concrete risks for this plan and goal, at most five, each one sentence. No em dashes.
- If the goal cannot be turned into a target, set "target_value" to 0 and explain in "risks".
